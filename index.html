Independent Application Fix Harlow is an independent, community-focused mobile application. It is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or directly operated by Harlow District Council, Essex County Council, North Essex Parking Partnership or Essex Police. While the app is designed to help citizens highlight local issues, it acts as an independent reporting and civic engagement platform. 2. No Guarantee of Action or Response Times Submitting a report through Fix Harlow does not guarantee that the reported issue will be investigated, prioritized, or resolved by local authorities, landowners, or third-party agencies. Responsibility for maintaining public roads, street furniture, waste collection, and council property lies with the relevant local government bodies or private landowners. Fix Harlow has no authority to compel repairs or interventions. 3. User Responsibility & Safety Do not endanger yourself or others while capturing photos or reporting issues (e.g., do not use your phone while driving, and do not trespass on private or dangerous property). Users are solely responsible for the content, text, and photographs they upload. You warrant that any photos submitted do not infringe upon the privacy rights, copyrights, or other rights of any third party. Do not upload images containing identifiable private individuals, offensive images or language, or sensitive personal data. Submitting prohibited content may result in an immediate and permanent ban of your account and associated email address. 4. Accuracy of Information Please ensure the information that you provide is accurate as possible to ensure that the local authorities can act efficiently with the information. 5. Emergency Situations Fix Harlow is NOT an emergency service. Do not use this app to report urgent matters that pose an immediate threat to life, property, or public safety (such as major accidents, crimes in progress, gas leaks, or dangerous live electrical wires). For all emergencies, please dial 999 immediately. Privacy Policy Welcome to Fix Harlow ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this privacy policy carefully. Information We Collect We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you use the application, submit reports, or contact us for support. Photos: When you upload photos of local issues, we collect these image files. 2. How We Use Your Information Having accurate information allows us to provide you with a smooth and efficient reporting experience. Specifically, we use information collected via Fix Harlow to: Create and log in a database to organise public reports of local issues for Harlow council. Respond to user inquiries and support requests. 3. Policy Regarding Children We do not knowingly solicit information from or market to children under the age of 13. If you become aware of any data we have collected from children under age 13, please contact us using the contact details provided below, and we will take steps to delete such information. 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date of this Privacy Policy. 7. Data Deletion & User Rights Although our app does not use traditional user accounts, we retain records of report submissions (including your name, email address, report location details, issue descriptions, and uploaded photos) to process fixes with Harlow Council. How to Request Data Erasure: You have the right to request the complete deletion of your personal data and report history at any time. To submit a request, please send an email to harlowlabs2@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request. Please include the email address you used when submitting your report. Data Retention Exceptions: Upon receiving your request, all associated personal details, report content, and uploaded photos will be permanently purged from our primary database and storage buckets. However, we retain anonymized cryptographic hashes of banned account identifiers strictly for platform security, fraud prevention, and enforcement of our blocklist. 8.
