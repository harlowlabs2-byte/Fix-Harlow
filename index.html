Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

This privacy policy applies to our issue reporting service for Harlow Council.

1. Data We Collect

When you submit a report, we collect:

  • Personal Information: Your Name and Email Address.
  • Report Details: Issue description, street name, postcode, landmark, and uploaded photos.
  • Device Identifiers: Internal user and device instance IDs generated to prevent spam and platform abuse.

Note: Location details are entered manually by you to pinpoint the report site. We do not access your device's GPS or background location.

2. How We Use & Share Your Data

Your details are securely stored in our backend (Supabase) to process your report and send a verification email. Once verified, report details (name, email, address, description, photos) are forwarded via email (Resend) to Harlow Council so they can inspect and resolve the issue.

3. Data Deletion Request

Although our app does not require a traditional account, you have the right to request full erasure of your personal data and report history at any time.
